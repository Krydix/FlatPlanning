## Storage zoning - V18

Window-side coat rail changed from 802 mm (96605) to the next smaller uncut 602 mm (96599), freeing 200 mm. Four coats now have about 140 mm pitch each, and bulky garments may require fewer. All table and middle shelf sizes retained. 1000 mm stock rail bay: two hanging rows at 950 and 1950 mm for short jackets, shirts and pullovers. 602 mm stock bay: four folded-clothes shelves under the top, two stacks per shelf. 602 mm stock coat bay: four more closely spaced long coats on a single 1950 mm rail, with a low shoe shelf. The existing wear-again throw-over rail remains in front of the folded bay. Overall top stays 2218 mm; room height is still assumed, not measured.

## Corrected shelf support

The desktop uses eight one-sided saddle holders; the other saddle-supported boards use four. Each holder wraps beneath a horizontal pipe. The shared shelf cross-pipes join two sliding T-connectors. The desk and speaker arms have a sliding-T root; their outer ends use a front-leg elbow return or a non-load-bearing end cover. No saddle is treated as an upright clamp; no split end or middle holders are assumed. Opposing saddles on shared cross-pipes are staggered so their straps do not overlap.

The three desk shelves share the wardrobe RF0/RB0 upright pair at the far end. The top cross-pipe is shared by the top desk shelf and first wardrobe shelf. The near shelf frame has two floor-mounted 1000 + 1200 mm stock uprights. These and the first wardrobe upright pair support the desktop through four T-connected stock arms: two 250 mm outward front arms and two 200 mm rear arms. Each front arm returns down through a 90 degree elbow into a 40 + 602 mm front leg. The two separate rear desktop legs are removed. All front supports terminate below the board, with no through-holes. Desktop stays 1615 x 702 mm in its V17 plan position; the top is now 732 mm, derived from stock lengths and preview fitting offsets. Eight saddle holders screw the board to the four arms. Two 250 mm low depth braces and two low wall ties link the reused shelf frame. Speaker trays remain independently supported at their V17 positions.

The Toom holder is the visual/reference product. Its exact tube fit, tab height and screw-hole offset have not been measured. The new `saddle_rise`, `saddle_tab_offset` and `saddle_half_width` fields remain null in Measurements-v8.json. The current previews use 15 / 32 / 7 mm respectively. Sample parts and cantilever clamp, floor/wall fixing and load checks are needed before final cuts.
