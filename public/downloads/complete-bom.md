## Connection and machining checks

Desk: a four-way cross at 689 mm on each middle and rear post. The middle cross takes the 250 mm front arm to the front-leg elbow and the 250 mm rear bridge; the rear cross takes that bridge and a 40 mm pipe into a wall flange under the back edge of the desktop. The earlier low braces at 235 and 400 mm are gone, so each front leg is held only at its elbow. Derived desktop underside 704 mm / top 732 mm.
Wall fixings: one flange per rear post at 1909 mm and two at 689 mm. Nothing from the pipe frame is fixed to the wall between 150 and 450 mm, where sockets and their feeds usually run. Services are still not surveyed: check the wall with a cable detector before drilling.
The four desktop post clearances and the two in the service shelf are 44 mm open-edge notches. Top boards finish at 2192 mm, before the crates.
Assembly: build each post from the floor up and turn every fitting to its direction as it goes on, then add the arms, rails and wall ties. Fittings are tightened to their direction, not to the end of the thread, so most joints need thread-locker. An uneven floor has to be taken up with shims under the floor flanges.
Fixed T, cross and elbow seating depths, the saddle holder, the end covers and all loads are unverified. The 60, 100, 150, 300 and 400 mm tubes and the threaded T are toom listings without a confirmed DOLLE number or price. If the real seating depth differs from the preview value, every level moves and the stacks must be re-solved.
Rear equipment shelf: 1615 x 230 x 18 mm, underside 500 / top 518 mm, 186 mm clear below the desktop. It begins 472 mm behind the desktop front; the middle knee zone remains open. It hangs on four separate shelf brackets fixed to the wall, 200 mm reach, not on the pipe frame: bracket type, size and wall fixings are an allowance still to be chosen.
M1 Mac mini 197 x 197 x 36 mm sits on the rear shelf with ports facing along its length for cable access. Router and power-brick sizes remain space allowances, not measured products. Keep the shelf open and separate the bricks; do not stack or strap across vents.
Focusrite and OWC dock use separate adjustable open-bottom/side U holders under the front ends. Their exposed front panels and cable reserves remain outside the 700 mm central seated knee width; bracket screw lengths and exact Focusrite generation remain to verify.

Cross seating offsets, exact physical assembly closure, fixings, clamp loads and room height require measurement. The current stock module only closes when measured cross/T/elbow stops match the profile constraints. No final drilling or routing template is supplied.
