Dependency notices are retained in the generated bundle where applicable.

DOLLE, IKEA, MITTLED, ÖVERSIDAN, TRÅDFRI and KNAGGLIG identify the referenced products. Supplier links and dated budget inputs are included in the BOM. Published ratings for other shelving systems are not treated as ratings for this custom arrangement.
